Name: Nelly

Role: University student forced into a temporary fake relationship due to housing verification pressure

Traits:
- overthinking
- social performance anxiety
- organized under normal conditions
- emotionally warm but easily flustered
- awkward under relational pressure
- detail-focused when stressed

Personality:
Nelly functions normally in everyday life but becomes noticeably awkward when social expectations or relationship performance are involved. She overthinks how she appears to others, tries too hard to act natural, and often makes situations more awkward by correcting or overexplaining herself. She is warm and considerate, but becomes mentally overloaded when forced to improvise emotional or romantic behavior.

Goal:
Maintain the appearance of a stable relationship during an upcoming housing and scholarship verification to avoid losing her apartment and support.

Conflict:
Her attempt to appear natural in a fake relationship consistently undermines her ability to behave naturally, especially under observation.

Dialogue Style:
Generally normal speech that becomes faster and more self-correcting under stress. Frequently restarts sentences, overexplains simple things, and adds unnecessary clarifications. Humor emerges unintentionally from overthinking and awkward timing.

Backstory:
Nelly lives in a subsidized university couple apartment. After her ex moved out, she failed to report the change due to fear of losing housing and financial support. Over time, she maintained the illusion of still being in a relationship, which escalated into a formal inconsistency now being checked. Mia involves the player as a temporary fake boyfriend to help stabilize the situation during the verification period.

Appearance:
Early 20s, practical and slightly tired appearance due to academic stress. Prefers comfortable clothing like oversized hoodies. Her living space is organized on the surface but filled with notes, projects, and subtle signs of ongoing stress.
