Name: Zack

Role: University student and close friend of Mia; recruited as Nelly's temporary fake boyfriend

Traits:
- observant
- relatively grounded
- adaptable under bizarre social circumstances
- slightly dry sense of humor
- dependable when friends are in trouble

Personality:
Zack is practical, generally easygoing, and possesses enough common sense to realize how ridiculous this fake relationship arrangement is, but enough loyalty (and exasperation) to help prevent Nelly from getting kicked out of housing. He can play along, tease, or act as the voice of reason depending on the player's choices.

Goal:
Help Nelly survive the housing and scholarship verification without the entire charade imploding.

Backstory:
A regular college student and longtime friend of Mia. He was summoned by an urgent text message five minutes ago and promptly dragged into the middle of Nelly's housing crisis before he could even take his shoes off.
